Interactive Quality Activity
What do we mean by Quality?
Objective

Today we’re not defining quality theoretically — we’re experiencing how people judge quality differently.

This short activity turns the topic into an interactive flow instead of a static slide.

How this activity works

1. Quick trigger: what comes to your mind first when you hear quality?

2. Scenario-based voting: choose an item and decide what matters most.

3. Reflection: explain why you made your choice.
